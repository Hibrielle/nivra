Nivra Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.0 · Updated October 20, 2026

Nivra manages and organizes browser downloads. Its developer is scieszkanottingham. Contact: scieszkanottingham@gmail.com.

Nivra has no developer-operated data collection server, advertisements, analytics SDK, or product telemetry. Most data stays in your browser. Features you choose to use, such as rule backups, resource loading, and external downloaders, communicate with the corresponding services.

Information processed

  • Download management: file names, local paths, source addresses, file types and sizes, timestamps, status, organization results, and your notes. Stored source URLs omit embedded credentials, query parameters, and fragments. Actions such as copying a link or downloading again read the complete URL from Chrome when needed. Pending downloads retain the original links needed to start them.
  • Resource discovery: after you select pages and start scanning or capture, Nivra extracts resource URLs, page titles, link text, content types, dimensions, and sizes. Network capture keeps relevant resource metadata from selected tabs, not request or response bodies. Image thumbnails load from the original websites, which may receive your IP address and cookies sent according to browser rules.
  • Download reports: transferred bytes, speed samples, pauses and failures, source and destination domains, observed redirects, and connection endpoint IP addresses. Nivra briefly observes HTTP(S) requests and fingerprints URLs to associate them with downloads. It does not look up DNS afterward or send reports to the developer. Endpoint IPs may belong to CDNs or proxies and are not used to infer your location.
  • Rules and preferences: matching conditions, folder and file name templates, filters, language, theme, and other settings. Rules can contain websites, keywords, or directory names you enter. They are evaluated locally without executing remote scripts.
  • External downloaders: if you connect one, Nivra stores its address, username, and password or RPC secret locally. When you send a torrent, it reads the corresponding .torrent file you select or authorize and submits its contents and name to your configured qBittorrent, Transmission, or aria2 service. Credentials are excluded from rule exports, cloud backups, reports, and MCP.

Storage and retention

Rules, settings, download records, the queue, and downloader connections use local Chrome extension storage. Reports use local IndexedDB. These rely on Chrome's extension isolation and your device's access controls; Nivra does not add application-level encryption to local storage. Automatic organization and reports do not record incognito downloads.

Nivra retains up to 500 finished task records, with optional cleanup by age. Original queue URLs are removed after completion or cancellation. Resource results remain in browser session storage until the corresponding tab closes or you clear them. The in-memory request association cache holds up to 256 entries; entries not updated for 30 seconds are pruned when later requests are processed, and the cache disappears when the background process stops.

Finished reports are retained for 30 days, up to 200 reports, under an approximately 8 MiB data budget. Each report holds at most 400 aggregated samples, 100 events, and 32 connections. Clearing a download history record or automatically removing its task also removes the report. Moving a task to Nivra's trash keeps its report.

Communication with other services

Downloads and resource thumbnails are requested from the websites you choose, subject to Chrome's and those sites' behavior. Only selecting “Upload and replace backup” writes your rules to Chrome Sync, which Google may sync to your other devices. Files, download records, reports, and downloader credentials are not included in rule backups.

Downloader credentials and torrent files are sent only to the service address you configure. Prefer HTTPS. HTTP does not encrypt the transfer and can expose its contents to other parties on the network. qBittorrent sign-in may create a session cookie managed by Chrome and that service; Nivra does not read or export cookie values.

The optional local MCP helper is not installed with the store extension. It processes rules supplied by its caller and does not read browser tasks or credentials. If you share a prompt, rule pack, or diagnostic summary with an AI service or another recipient, that recipient handles the information you choose to provide.

Your controls

You can view tasks, reports, rules, and settings in the manager; remove records through task actions; clear resource results; and disconnect an external downloader in settings. Deleting a local file is a separate action requiring confirmation. Clearing plugin records does not automatically remove files from disk.

Use Settings → Cloud rule backup → Delete cloud backup to remove the synced backup without changing local rules. Uninstalling Nivra removes local extension data. Downloaded files, Chrome's download history, remote downloader tasks, and remote login sessions must be managed separately. Optional notification, file-opening, and local-file permissions can be revoked in Chrome's extension settings.

Limited use and contact

Nivra's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data is used only for the user-facing features described above. It is not sold, used for advertising, creditworthiness assessments, or lending, or made available for the developer or others to inspect on your device. Information you voluntarily provide when requesting support is used to address that request.

We will update this policy if our data practices change and explain features that need additional authorization before they are enabled. For privacy questions, contact scieszkanottingham@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, RPC secrets, or download links containing access tokens.

Nivra 隐私政策

适用版本：1.0.0 · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 20 日

Nivra 是一个管理和整理浏览器下载的扩展。开发者为 scieszkanottingham，联系邮箱为 scieszkanottingham@gmail.com。

Nivra 没有开发者运营的数据收集服务器，不含广告、分析 SDK 或产品遥测。主要数据保存在你的浏览器中；你主动使用规则云端备份、网页资源加载或外部下载器时，会与相应服务通信。

处理哪些信息

  • 下载管理：文件名、本地保存路径、来源地址、文件类型与大小、下载时间、状态、归档结果及你填写的备注。插件持久保存的来源地址去除账号密码、查询参数和片段；复制链接、重新下载等操作会按需从 Chrome 下载记录读取完整链接。待下载队列为发起下载保留原始链接。
  • 资源发现：只有你选择网页并点击扫描或开启捕获后，才提取该网页中的资源链接、页面标题、链接文字、资源类型、尺寸、大小等信息。网络捕获只保留所选标签页中用于识别下载资源的响应信息，不保存请求或响应正文。展示图片缩略图会向原资源网站加载图片，网站可能收到 IP 地址及浏览器按其规则携带的 Cookie。
  • 下载报告：下载字节数、速度采样、暂停与失败事件、来源与最终域名、观测到的重定向及连接端点 IP。为关联下载，扩展短暂观察 HTTP(S) 请求，将链接转换为指纹；不会事后查询 DNS，也不会将这些报告上传给开发者。端点 IP 可能属于 CDN 或代理，不用于推断用户位置。
  • 规则和偏好：你设置的分类条件、目录模板、筛选器、主题及其他偏好。规则可能包含你填写的网站、关键词或目录名称。规则在本机计算，不执行远端脚本。
  • 外部下载器：只有你主动绑定时，保存服务地址、用户名和密码或 RPC 密钥；主动发送种子时读取你选择或授权访问的对应 .torrent 文件，并将其内容和文件名交给你配置的 qBittorrent、Transmission 或 aria2。凭据不进入规则导出、云端备份、下载报告或 MCP。

数据保存在何处、保留多久

规则、偏好、下载任务、队列及下载器绑定保存在本机 Chrome 扩展存储中；下载报告保存在本机 IndexedDB。这些数据依赖 Chrome 的扩展隔离和设备访问控制，Nivra 不额外加密本地存储。自动归档和报告不记录无痕下载。

已结束的插件任务最多保留 500 条；你也可以设置按天自动清理。待下载队列在完成或取消后移除原始链接。资源发现结果保存在浏览器会话中，关闭对应标签页或手动清空时移除。请求关联缓存保留在内存中，最多 256 项，在处理后续请求时淘汰超过 30 秒未更新的项目，后台停止时释放。

已结束报告保留 30 天、最多 200 份，按约 8 MiB 数据预算清理较旧报告；每份最多 400 个采样点、100 个事件和 32 条连接。清除对应下载历史或自动清理插件任务时，也会清理其报告；移入插件回收站会保留报告。

何时与其他服务通信

下载与资源缩略图请求会发送给你选择的原网站，按 Chrome 和该网站的规则处理。只有你点击“上传并替换云端备份”时，规则才写入 Chrome 同步存储，并可能由 Google 同步到你的其他设备。下载文件、任务记录、报告与下载器凭据不参与规则同步。

外部下载器凭据与种子文件只发送到你配置的服务地址。请优先使用 HTTPS；HTTP 传输不加密，可能被同一网络中的其他参与者读取。qBittorrent 登录可能产生由浏览器和该服务管理的会话 Cookie；扩展不读取或导出 Cookie 值。

可选的本地 MCP 工具不随商店安装包安装，只处理调用方主动提供的规则，不读取浏览器任务或凭据。你将提示词、规则或报告摘要提供给 AI 服务或其他人时，信息由你选择的服务或接收者处理。

如何查看、删除和控制

你可以在管理页查看任务、报告、规则和偏好；在任务菜单中移除记录或清除浏览器下载历史，在资源发现中清空结果，在设置中解除下载器绑定。删除本地文件是独立操作，需要确认；清理插件记录不会自动删除磁盘文件。

在“设置 → 规则云端备份”可以删除云端备份，本机规则保持不变。卸载扩展会清除本机扩展数据；已经下载到磁盘的文件、Chrome 下载历史、外部下载器中的任务及远端登录会话需在各自位置另行管理。通知、打开文件和本地文件访问可以在 Chrome 扩展权限中撤销。

使用范围与联系

Nivra 对从 Google API 接收到的信息的使用遵循 Chrome Web Store 用户数据政策，包括 Limited Use 要求。数据仅用于上述面向用户的功能，不出售数据，不用于广告、信用评估或放贷，也不允许开发者或其他人员主动查看你的本机数据。你主动请求技术支持并自愿提供的信息，仅用于处理该请求。

如果未来改变数据处理方式，我们会更新本政策；需要新增授权的功能会在启用前说明。隐私相关问题请联系 scieszkanottingham@gmail.com。请不要发送密码、RPC 密钥或含访问令牌的下载链接。
