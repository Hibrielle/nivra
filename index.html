Nivra

Downloads,
organized.

Manage downloads in one place, put new files in the right folders, and look back at their speed and connection details.

View source on GitHub Privacy policy / 隐私政策

A familiar download manager

Pause, resume, retry, copy links, and find files from a compact popup or the full manager.

Your folders, your rules

Combine file types with monthly or daily folders. Preview paths before saving custom rules.

Reports on your device

Inspect sampled speeds, pauses, and observed connection IPs. No product telemetry or developer data server.

English and Chinese

Follows your browser language, with a manual choice in settings. Light and dark themes are included.

Version 1.0.0 is being prepared for the Chrome Web Store. Requires Chrome 127 or later. Source code is available under the MIT license.
